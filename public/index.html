The Science of Scent Progression

The Olfactory Pyramid

True haute perfumery evolves in 3 harmonious stages as it warms against your skin.

Top Notes

First 15 – 30 Minutes

The immediate sensory crescendo. Radiant Calabrian bergamot, saffron filaments, and sparkling Italian citrus that enchant the air instantly upon application.

Heart Notes

Hours 1 – 5

The soul and identity of the composition. Grandiflorum jasmine, midnight Damascus rose, French lavender, and smoky cedarwood revealing the true character.

Base Notes

Hours 6 – 24+

The immortal foundation. Rare Mysore sandalwood, velvety ambergris, Cambodian oud, and Mexican bourbon vanilla lingering forever on fabrics and memory.
